## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional collegiate identity refined by the precision and physical clarity of modern iOS Human Interface Guidelines. Crafted for advanced K-12 School Management Systems, it departs from generic corporate blue SaaS templates in favor of a warm, timeless academic palette anchored in rich espresso tones, unbleached book-stock ivory, and deliberate caramel highlights.

The aesthetic evokes leather-bound ledgers, collegiate libraries, and premier academic administration without sacrificing modern, high-velocity data ergonomics. The experience balances administrative authority with calming, high-legibility hospitality:
- **Tone & Personality:** Authoritative, hospitable, meticulous, intellectual, and dignified.
- **Visual Movement:** Tactile Warm Minimalist combined with iOS grouped-inset table ergonomics and editorial typography.
- **Target Audience:** School principals, academic registrars, department heads, educators, parents, and trustees requiring deep focus across dense schedules, student records, and financial ledgers.
- **Color Distribution Principle:** 70% Warm Cream & Ivory base canvases, 20% Espresso & Mocha foundational structure, and 10% Caramel accents for focused achievements and critical indicators.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to provide long-session visual comfort while maintaining strict WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios across all administrative tables, transcript sheets, and scheduling tools.

### Brand Tokens & Surfaces
- **Primary Deep Espresso (`#3E2A22`):** Used for root structural anchors, dark high-emphasis headers, active pressed states, and primary navigational selection.
- **Espresso Core (`#5C4033`):** The operational workhorse color for primary call-to-actions, focus halos, interactive links, and verified student status tags.
- **Secondary Mocha (`#7A5C48`):** Mid-tone supporting chrome, structural icons, sub-navigation tabs, and secondary analytical charts.
- **Accent Caramel (`#C58B5A`):** Used strictly at 10% volume for honors recognitions, key performance indicators, active badges, and achievement ribbons.
- **Canvas Warm Cream (`#F7F1E8`):** The global viewport background, providing warmth and eye relief compared to harsh stark white.
- **Surface Ivory (`#FFFDF9`):** Elevated work surfaces, bento cards, student record detail sheets, and grouped inset forms.
- **Border Soft Beige (`#E5D8CA`):** Structural boundaries, hairline dividers, and inactive input field outlines.

### Text Hierarchy
- **Primary Text (Dark Cocoa, `#2D211B`):** 13.8:1 contrast over Surface Ivory for effortless reading of dense data.
- **Secondary Text (Taupe, `#8C7A6B`):** Supporting metadata, grade indicators, time stamps, and form label descriptors.

### Functional Semantics
- **Success:** Forest (`#2E7D4F`) on Mint Mist (`#EAF4ED`) — Passing grades, attendance verified, payments received.
- **Warning:** Amber Ochre (`#A66A00`) on Cream Gold (`#FFF4D6`) — Pending approvals, disciplinary alerts, academic probation warnings.
- **Danger:** Venetian Brick (`#B5443C`) on Blush (`#FBEAE8`) — Unexcused absences, system errors, critical balance past due.
- **Info:** Slate Marine (`#35758A`) on Ice Teal (`#E8F3F6`) — Informational notices, curriculum updates, general notices.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial cadence. The pairing pairs **Newsreader**—an authoritative, scholarly serif with optical weight balance—with **Manrope**—a precise, geometric yet warm sans-serif optimized for tabular screens and iOS layout standards.

### Typographic Rules
- **Editorial Voice:** Section headers, student transcript banners, and major metrics utilize `Newsreader` in medium weights to communicate institutional trust.
- **Functional Clarity:** All forms, tables, data rows, UI chips, and navigation links exclusively utilize `Manrope`.
- **Numerics:** Manrope handles all tabular data, timestamps, grade calculations, and GPA values with proportional or tabular figures enabled to prevent jitter across re-renders.
- **Hierarchy Distinction:** Titles and section markers in `Newsreader` must always be paired with secondary descriptions in `Manrope` `body-md` in Secondary Text Taupe (`#8C7A6B`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an **iOS Inset Grouped** structural model adapted for responsive web applications:
- **Global Canvas:** Pages reside on a Warm Cream (`#F7F1E8`) backdrop with content grouped into elevated Ivory (`#FFFDF9`) modules.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column adaptive grid with a fixed collapsible 260px administrative navigation drawer, 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) external canvas margins. Bento widgets and grouped sections stretch over 3, 4, 6, or 12 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Navigation condenses to an adaptive sidebar or rail.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout using native iOS edge margins (16px / `1rem`), with 12px (`0.75rem`) gaps between inset list items and cards.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm governs all layout paddings (`space-sm` for badge interiors, `space-md` for row spacing, `space-lg` for card internal padding, `space-xl` for dashboard module separation).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is achieved through **tonal stratification and warm diffused ambient shadows**, eschewing cold grey drops in favor of espresso-tinted ambient occlusion.

### The Stack Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#F7F1E8`. Contains page title sequences, breadcrumbs, and inactive background space.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Inset Groups):** `#FFFDF9` with a crisp hairline border of `1px solid #E5D8CA`. Shadow: `0px 2px 8px rgba(62, 42, 34, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Segmented Dropdowns):** `#FFFDF9` with `1px solid #D8C7B5`. Shadow: `0px 6px 16px rgba(62, 42, 34, 0.07)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Popovers, Action Sheets):** `#FFFDF9` with `1px solid #D8C7B5`. Shadow: `0px 12px 32px rgba(62, 42, 34, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Toasts & Floating Action Bars):** Primary Espresso `#3E2A22` filled pill surface, light text, with `0px 8px 24px rgba(45, 33, 27, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on iOS-grade curvature calibrated through `roundedness: 2`:
- **Core Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Tabs):** `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, inputs, and chips, creating a friendly yet organized administrative interface.
- **Cards, Bento Modules, Grouped Inset Panels:** `1rem` (16px) bounding radius to mimic iOS system widgets and native inset grouped table views.
- **Modals & Bottom Drawers:** `1.5rem` (24px) for full-view focal overlays.
- **Pills:** Full rounded pill geometry is reserved exclusively for numerical count badges, status indicators, and quick filter chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with `#5C4033`, text in `#FFFDF9`, border radius `0.5rem`. Height: 42px desktop, 44px mobile (iOS tap target). Hover: `#3E2A22`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#C58B5A`.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFDF9` with `1px solid #E5D8CA`, text in `#3E2A22`. Hover: `#F7F1E8`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No fill or border, text in `#7A5C48`. Hover: `#F7F1E8` fill, text `#3E2A22`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FBEAE8`, text `#B5443C`. Hover: `#B5443C` filled, text white.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, background `#FFFDF9`, border `1px solid #E5D8CA`, radius `0.5rem`, typography `body-md` in `#2D211B`. Placeholder in `#8C7A6B`. Active/Focus state uses border `#5C4033` with `0 0 0 3px rgba(92, 64, 51, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px diameter/width, border `1.5px solid #8C7A6B`. Checked state fills `#5C4033` with an ivory tick. Radio items contain an inner dot of `#FFFDF9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Academic Honors Badge:** Background `#FFF4D6`, text `#A66A00`, hairline border `1px solid rgba(197, 139, 90, 0.3)`.
- **Filter Chips:** Height 32px, pill-shaped, background `#F7F1E8`, text `#7A5C48`. When selected: background `#3E2A22`, text `#FFFDF9`.

### Lists & Grouped Tables
- Modeled on iOS Inset Grouped views. Container uses `#FFFDF9` with `1rem` corner rounding and `1px solid #E5D8CA`.
- Rows feature 52px minimum height, partitioned by `1px solid #F7F1E8` dividers indented 16px to clear leading avatars or subject icons.
- Row tap effect creates an instant `#F7F1E8` highlight.

### Cards & Bento Widgets
- Inset cards with `1rem` radius, Ivory fill, soft beige border, and Level 1 elevation.
- Bento header areas utilize `Newsreader` `headline-sm` with action buttons right-aligned in secondary text sizing.

### Domain-Specific Components (K-12 SMS)
- **Student Profile Capsule:** 64px compact horizontal row grouping an avatar with warm espresso border, primary name in `title-md`, grade/homeroom tag in a mocha pill badge, and rapid contact triggers.
- **Attendance Marker Matrix:** Segmented multi-state toggle (Present, Tardy, Excused, Absent) using semantic green, ochre, info teal, and brick fills with soft iOS spring transitions.
- **Academic Grade Ribbon:** Compact card displaying student letter grade rendered in `Newsreader` `headline-xl` anchored with percentage progress track in Caramel (`#C58B5A`).

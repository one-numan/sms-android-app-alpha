## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional collegiate identity refined by the precision and physical clarity of modern mobile and inset ergonomics. Crafted for school ERP and educational administration applications, it departs from sterile corporate blue SaaS interfaces in favor of a warm, timeless academic palette anchored in rich espresso tones, unbleached book-stock ivory, and warm caramel accents.

The visual language evokes collegiate libraries, leather-bound ledgers, and premier academic offices while maintaining modern, touch-friendly administrative efficiency:
- **Tone & Personality:** Authoritative, hospitable, meticulous, intellectual, and dignified.
- **Visual Movement:** Tactile warm minimalism combined with inset grouped table ergonomics, crisp hairline partitions, and refined editorial typography.
- **Target Audience:** School principals, academic registrars, teachers, parents, and administrative staff requiring extended focus across dense student rosters, transcripts, and financial ledgers.
- **Color Distribution Principle:** 70% unbleached ivory and warm parchment canvases, 20% espresso structural anchors, and 10% caramel accents for scholastic achievements and key milestones.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for visual comfort during prolonged administrative sessions, balancing institutional warmth with WCAG 2.1 AA compliant contrast across records, tables, and forms within a sophisticated light mode environment.

### Brand Surfaces & Chrome
- **Primary Espresso (`#5C4033`):** The primary brand anchor used for primary action buttons, focused controls, active navigation items, and verified indicators.
- **Deep Espresso (`#3E2A22`):** Grounding tone for navigation bars, active pressed states, and high-emphasis focal headers.
- **Secondary Mocha (`#765844`):** Supporting chrome, segment controls, inactive icons, and analytical chart series.
- **Tertiary Caramel (`#C58B5A`):** Used at roughly 10% surface volume for scholastic honors, grade indicators, achievement badges, and active progress gauges.
- **Canvas Cream (`#FBF9F7`):** The default viewport background, providing a warm, non-glare surface tailored for light mode environments.
- **Surface Ivory (`#FFFFFF`):** Elevated card containers, inset grouped lists, form sheets, and dialog surfaces.
- **Border Beige (`#E6DFDC`):** Low-contrast structural borders, card strokes, and hairline list row dividers.

### Text Contrast Hierarchy
- **Text Cocoa (`#2B221E`):** High-contrast primary reading tone across titles, ledger figures, and student names.
- **Text Taupe (`#817470`):** Muted secondary copy, metadata tags, field placeholders, and helper annotations.

### Functional Semantics
- **Success (`#2E7D4F` / `#E8F5E9`):** Attendance confirmation, passing marks, and cleared fee accounts.
- **Warning (`#A66A00` / `#FFF8E1`):** Disciplinary flags, pending parental approvals, and probation advisories.
- **Danger (`#B5443C` / `#FFEBEE`):** Unexcused absences, past-due payments, and destructive operations.
- **Info (`#35758A` / `#E0F7FA`):** Academic notices, timetable revisions, and system updates.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes an editorial dynamic between two distinct voices:
1. **Newsreader (Editorial & Collegiate Titles):** Conveys academic heritage, scholarship, and administrative authority across transcript banners, report card headings, and section titling.
2. **Manrope (Tabular Precision & UI):** Delivers clean legibility for dense ERP interfaces, attendance registers, timetable slots, forms, and numerical indicators.

### Implementation Guidelines
- **Tabular Numerics:** Numerical figures (grade percentages, GPA values, tuition balances, timestamps) must be rendered using `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` in Manrope to align perfectly in dense data grids.
- **Scale Pairing:** Pair `Newsreader` headlines with `Manrope` `body-md` secondary subtitles set in Text Taupe (`#817470`) to avoid visual conflict between serif and sans-serif weights.
- **Mobile Adaptations:** Headings larger than 28px scale down gracefully using dedicated mobile scales (`display-lg-mobile`, `headline-xl-mobile`) to prevent awkward line breaks in narrow mobile cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adapts the iOS Inset Grouped structural archetype for mobile ERP viewports and responsive management consoles:
- **Canvas Base:** Content sits on an edge-to-edge Light Canvas (`#FBF9F7`) ground, separating discrete administrative actions into distinct elevated Surface Ivory (`#FFFFFF`) modules.
- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop and tablet dashboards follow an adaptive 12-column or 8-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) outer margins. Handheld mobile screens collapse into a single-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) horizontal outer margins and 12px (`0.75rem`) gaps between stacked card groups.
- **Rhythm Scale:** Built upon an 8-point base scale with 4-point micro-adjustments:
  - `space-xs` (4px): Inner badge padding, icon-to-label gaps.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Form input inner vertical gaps, chip paddings.
  - `space-md` (16px): Standard card interior paddings, table row separations.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Primary modal padding, section separations within forms.
  - `space-xl` (36px): Top-level dashboard module spacing and page title clearances.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses light-mode tonal layering and crisp shadow casting rather than flat outlines, reinforcing the physical metaphor of archival parchment surfaces layered with precision lighting.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat Light Canvas (`#FBF9F7`). Contains screen headers, global status bars, and negative framing space.
- **Level 1 (Inset Cards & Roster Groups):** Surface Ivory (`#FFFFFF`) with a hairline border of `1px solid #E6DFDC` and subtle shadow `0px 2px 6px rgba(92, 64, 51, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Dropdowns):** Surface Ivory (`#FFFFFF`) with `1px solid #E6DFDC` and shadow `0px 6px 16px rgba(92, 64, 51, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Student Sheets, Action Sheets):** Surface Ivory (`#FFFFFF`) bounded by `1px solid #E6DFDC` and shadow `0px 16px 36px rgba(92, 64, 51, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Floating Action Bars & Snackbars):** Deep Espresso (`#3E2A22`) with Surface Ivory text and shadow `0px 8px 24px rgba(92, 64, 51, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

With `roundedness: 2`, components adopt an organic, calibrated geometry mirroring native inset grouped views and contemporary mobile design languages:
- **Small Controls (Buttons, Text Inputs, Search Bars):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius ensures actionable clarity and precise form alignment.
- **Grouped Panels, Roster Cards, Bento Modules:** `1rem` (16px) radius for grouped inset card collections and academic detail views.
- **Overlays (Modals, Bottom Drawers):** `1.5rem` (24px) corner radius along top edges for mobile pull sheets.
- **Pill Badges:** Fully rounded `9999px` geometry strictly reserved for attendance markers, status chips, GPA badges, and numerical counts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Espresso (`#5C4033`) fill with Surface Ivory (`#FFFFFF`) text, `0.5rem` corner radius, 44px touch target height. Pressed state transitions to Deep Espresso (`#3E2A22`). Focus ring is a 2px offset in Caramel (`#C58B5A`).
- **Secondary Action:** Surface Ivory (`#FFFFFF`) fill with `1px solid #E6DFDC` border and Text Cocoa (`#2B221E`) text. Active state shifts to Light Canvas (`#FBF9F7`).
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background with Secondary Mocha (`#765844`) text, hovering or pressing triggers a subtle `#E6DFDC` fill.
- **Destructive:** Light blush (`#FFEBEE`) fill with Brick (`#B5443C`) text and `1px solid rgba(181, 68, 60, 0.3)`.

### Form Inputs
- **Text & Select Fields:** 44px touch height, Surface Ivory (`#FFFFFF`) background, `1px solid #E6DFDC` border, `0.5rem` radius, and Manrope `body-md` typography. Placeholders render in Text Taupe (`#817470`).
- **Focus State:** Border changes to Espresso (`#5C4033`) backed by an ambient glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(92, 64, 51, 0.15)`.
- **Validation Messages:** Positioned 4px below field in Brick (`#B5443C`) with Manrope `caption` weight.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Controls:** 20px box/circle, `1.5px solid #817470` default outline.
- **Selected State:** Filled with Espresso (`#5C4033`) presenting an Ivory checkmark icon or an inner Ivory dot (`#FFFFFF`).

### Lists & Inset Grouped Tables
- Containers match the iOS Inset Grouped model: Surface Ivory (`#FFFFFF`) background, `1rem` outer radius, and `1px solid #E6DFDC` perimeter border.
- Rows maintain a minimum 52px height, separated by hairline dividers in `#FBF9F7` indented 16px to align with text labels past avatars or course glyphs.
- Active tap states trigger an instantaneous `#FBF9F7` feedback overlay.

### Academic ERP Specialized Components
- **Student Profile Capsule:** Horizontal card featuring a circular student avatar framed in Deep Espresso, primary student name in `title-md`, homeroom/grade badge in Mocha pill format, and rapid action triggers (call, message, profile view).
- **Attendance Marker Matrix:** Segmented multi-state toggle (P: Present, T: Tardy, E: Excused, A: Absent) utilizing light semantic fills on light background tints with smooth spring feedback.
- **Academic Grade Ribbon:** Inset card displaying the letter grade in Newsreader `headline-xl` accompanied by a horizontal percentage meter filled in Caramel (`#C58B5A`).
- **Fee Ledger Row:** Monospaced tabular figures in Manrope displaying invoice dates, balance amounts, and semantic status chips (Paid, Pending, Overdue).
